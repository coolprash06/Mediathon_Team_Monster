Files were resized / recompressed. Colour is re-tinted in CSS with
background-blend-mode, so one scan can serve several materials
(e.g. painted-metal.jpg is both the olive cabinets and the beige pedestals).

These are surface materials only. Every PHOTOGRAPH shown in the portfolio
itself (monitor slideshows, drawer files) must be the team's own shots from
the competition window, with EXIF intact - see guidelines.txt.
